A proposed partnership for electrical protection and automation of substations and industrial facilities in Libya.

From one pilot project to a locally supported, multi-site modernisation programme.

What does electrical protection do?

Detect

Monitor electrical conditions and identify faults.

Protect

Command circuit breakers to isolate the faulty section according to an engineered scheme.

Understand

Record events and provide operators with measurements and alarms.

Protection does not generate electricity or eliminate every cause of outages.

Relevant to oil and gas

Industrial substations, pump and compressor drives, transformers, motors and on-site generation require appropriate electrical protection and control.

Cheboksary manufacturers publish existing product families for these applications. EKRA has presented protection, automation, industrial UPS and switchgear for the oil-and-gas sector.

Every facility requires engineering, interface checks and owner approval. Hazardous-area requirements must be separately specified. This is not an offer of process-control or explosion-proof equipment for every application.

One project. A defined scope.

Assess

Review diagrams, installed equipment and operational needs.

Engineer & supply

Specify devices, cabinets, drawings, settings and tests.

Commission & support

Agree qualified installation, site tests, training and maintenance responsibilities.

Engineered for actual site conditions

Specify heat, dust, coastal humidity and corrosion exposure. Verify indoor temperatures, enclosure protection, filtration, ventilation and cooling requirements.

We do not claim all products are already adapted to every Libyan site. Written manufacturer confirmation of operating limits, configuration and warranty is required.

Explore Russian export financing

Russian export-credit instruments can be assessed alongside the technical proposal. The objective is an agreed payment structure for the supplier and a bank-approved payment schedule for the buyer.

Buyer qualification → technical scope → bank assessment → financing terms → delivery.

No Libya facility, rate, tenor or bank participation is approved or promised. Country eligibility, buyer credit, security, currency, payment route, compliance and insurance must be checked. RosEXIMBANK publicly lists financing for foreign purchases of Russian exports.

2. Complete and document acceptance.

This is a proposed development path, not an awarded national programme, government mandate or exclusive representation.
